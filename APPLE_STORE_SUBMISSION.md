## 📝 App Description

### Promotional Text - Short Description (170 characters)

### Full Description

**Quote Wisdom – Daily Inspiration You’ll Actually Keep**

Grow a personal sanctuary of wisdom, humor, and motivation. Quote Wisdom serves up 300 carefully curated quotes, lets you save the ones you love, and keeps favorite mode ready for when you need a lift.

**KEY FEATURES:**

 **Daily Highlights & Shuffle**
- 300 preloaded quotes across motivation, love, joy, and reflection
- Smart shuffling avoids recently seen quotes
- Morning-ready design with large typography and calming gradients

 **Favorite Mode**
- Heart any quote to build your own inspiration shelf
- Flip into favorite mode to browse only the lines you’ve saved
- Keep track of every favorite with timestamps for easy revisits

 **Quote Explorer**
- Discover quotes by searching text or author
- Switch between all quotes and favorites instantly
- Pull to refresh for brand-new combinations

 **Readers & Profiles**
- Add multiple readers so every person keeps their own favorites
- Effortlessly switch profiles without losing anyone’s list
- All data stored securely on-device (no sign-in required)

 **Designed for Calm**
- Gradient cards, gentle animations, and readable type
- Encouraging copy and delightful empty states
- Works perfectly offline—ideal for mindful breaks

**Perfect for:**
- Morning rituals and bedtime reflection
- Journaling prompts and gratitude practice
- Team standups, classrooms, and group inspiration

*Shuffle when you need discovery. Switch to favorite mode when you want comfort.*
